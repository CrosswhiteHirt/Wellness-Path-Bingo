Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Wellness Path Bingo (“the App,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the App. By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described below.

1. Information We Handle

The App stores gameplay preferences and progress locally on your device, including sound settings, selected difficulty, completed challenges, learned habit identifiers, best times, stars, and accuracy records.

The App and its service providers may also process:

  • Device and app information, such as device type, operating system, app version, bundle identifier, language, and general technical diagnostics.
  • Device identifiers, including the Identifier for Vendor (IDFV) and, only when permitted by you through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt, the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Advertising events, ad interactions, approximate network information, and fraud-prevention or security signals.
  • Network request information needed to retrieve advertising configuration and provide rewarded advertising.

2. App Tracking Transparency

On supported Apple devices, the App asks for permission before accessing the IDFA or tracking your activity across other companies’ apps and websites. You may deny this request. You can change your choice at any time in your device’s Settings. Core gameplay remains available if you decline tracking.

3. How Information Is Used

  • To operate the game, save settings, and maintain progress on your device.
  • To provide, measure, and improve rewarded advertising.
  • To configure third-party advertising services.
  • To protect the App, detect misuse, and support technical security.
  • To diagnose performance and reliability issues.

4. Third-Party Services

The App may use advertising and related technology supplied by third parties, including Chartboost, Yandex Mobile Ads, Vungle/Liftoff Monetize, and InMobi. These providers may process information under their own privacy policies. Their availability may vary by region and by the App’s current advertising configuration.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your directly identifying personal information. Information may be shared with service providers that support advertising, analytics, configuration, security, and technical operation; when required by law; or when necessary to protect users, our rights, or the integrity of the App.

6. Data Retention

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you reset the relevant settings, remove the data, or uninstall the App. Third-party providers may retain information according to their own policies and legal obligations.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request names, addresses, or other directly identifying contact information from children through the game. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can review the matter.

8. International Processing

Third-party service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, information is handled using appropriate legal safeguards.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

10. Your Choices and Rights

You may control tracking permission in Apple device settings, reset or remove local game data by clearing the App’s data or uninstalling it, and contact us with privacy questions or requests. Additional rights may apply depending on your location.

11. Health Disclaimer

The App provides general educational wellness content only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a qualified professional regarding medical or health concerns.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the App, legal requirements, or service providers. The revised policy will show an updated effective date.
